How we researched it

Two analyses that anyone could repeat

A · Old newspapers

Trove Newspapers, articles only: results per decade, 1850s–1950s

Rowing: sculling OR "rowing club"

Swimming: "swimming club" OR "swimming carnival"

Surfing: "surf bathing" OR "surf club" OR surfboard

Check: opened 10 results per sport to count how many were really about it

B · Today’s numbers

Each sport’s share of three measures, 2024–26

Participation: AusPlay 2024–25, adults

Funding: ASC high-performance investment, 2025–26

Medals: Paris 2024, from Olympedia

Compared as broad shares, not raw totals

Literature: three sources per topic from the USyd Library, SPORTDiscus and JSTOR, read for each author’s main claim
