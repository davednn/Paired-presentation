DAVE · 1:15 The argument in one line: pools and beaches have excluded people, and national myths borrowed Pacific Islander skill. Name authors out loud: "Curthoys, who was on the Freedom Ride…", "Osmond argues…", "Noble's collection on Cronulla…" (one line each, from your reading). Rowing: we found almost no research on race and rowing. Say so: it is a gap worth naming. Swimming: Aboriginal children were kept out of the Moree baths until the Freedom Ride in 1965; the "Australian crawl" story leaves out the Pacific Islander swimmers it came from. Surfing: Duke Kahanamoku's Freshwater demonstration, December 1914, did not introduce board riding; others had demonstrated it earlier (Osmond). Cronulla, 2005: the beach as a site of exclusion. If asked about Indigenous perspectives: water as Country predates all three sports; we stick to published scholarship rather than speak for communities.
